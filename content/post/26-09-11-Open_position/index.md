The Nano-Optics & Biophysics Laboratory in the Department of Electrophysics at National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University (NYCU) is seeking a highly motivated candidate for a <b>full-time Research Assistant</b> position.
Our laboratory develops <b>advanced optical microscopy technologies</b> and integrates them with chemical and biological approaches to quantitatively investigate <b>nanoscale structures and dynamics in living cells</b>. 
We are particularly interested in understanding <b>nanoscale cell biophysics</b>, with a focus on cellular dynamics in living cells. Our overarching goal is to resolve the organization and dynamics of biomolecules at the nanoscale and to elucidate their relationships with cellular functions <b>using label-free approaches</b>.

<!--more-->
Qualifications
Applicants should have a background in biophysics, biomedical engineering, life sciences, or a related field. Experience in optical microscopy, biological sample preparation (cell culture), or cell-based experiments is preferred. Candidates should demonstrate independent thinking, and teamwork skills.

Salary
The salary will be provided according to NYCU salary standards. Starting monthly salary is NT$43,965 for Master’s degree holders, and NT$39,180 for Bachelor’s degree holders, subject to adjustment based on education and experience. This position is covered by the Labor Standards Act.
